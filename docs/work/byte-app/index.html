The short version

A redesign of the Byte food delivery app to be easier to use and look more contemporary.

01 / ContextAbout the client

Byte Technology Systems was a Y-Combinator funded food delivery start-up based in Lahore, Pakistan. The aim of this project was to re-design the whole Byte app to make it easier in terms of usability and to make it look more visually appealing and contemporary.
