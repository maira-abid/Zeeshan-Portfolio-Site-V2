The short version

CarSwitch had to make a boring process interesting. Three humorous animations cut social media queries by 30% and lifted website visits by 50%.

01 / ContextAbout the client

Carswitch is a Dubai (UAE) based brand that enables you to buy or sell a used car without the worry and hassle of any paperwork. It steers you clear of any shady transactions or sketchy conversations which may lead you to sell your car for a lesser value or buy a “lemon car”. My role in the project was to use playful and humorous visuals together with its branding to create these animations. The purpose of the animations was to showcase the unique features and benefits that users can enjoy with peace of mind when they use CarSwitch.

WatchThe Animations

02 / The challengeWhat needed solving

CarSwitch wanted to market its services and the unique advantages they offer to both vehicle buyers and sellers. For sellers, CarSwitch handles all of the problems, process and paperwork that come with selling the car. Sellers can expect to get the selling price that they want and not lose value of their car. For buyers, CarSwitch inspects and tests the cars. This way buyers know that they're not being sold "lemon cars". They wanted to highlight how and why all of these services set them apart from other brands in Dubai's used car market and that they offer a complete solution from beginning to end. Customers do not need to put in effort and instead CarSwitch does the work for them.

03 / My roleWhat I did

I started by first understanding the client's business model and their requirements for the animations including the message they wanted to get across. I was at the forefront of communicating with the client throughout the entire project. At every stage, I made sure to incorporate the client's feedback into the animations without compromising on the quality and aesthetic of the brand.

04 / ApproachHow I worked it out

My process started with research on the brand's mission, vision, values and personality which I then used to design the art direction. Presenting the storyboard to the client before starting work on the animation made sure that they had a clear idea of how the animation would look once it was complete. I also worked together with social media managers and project managers and the client. However, I also had to adhere to the constraints put around the animations (such as a limited duration) as they were to be used for YouTube ads as well.

05 / ResultsWhat happened

These animations that used humour to explain CarSwitch's car selling and buying process; a process that was usually thought to be very boring. They communicated all of CarSwitch's services and benefits without coming off as an ad trying to sell a product or service while still adhering to the requirements of the brand, the client and the time limits.
