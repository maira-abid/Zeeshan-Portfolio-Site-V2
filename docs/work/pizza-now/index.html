Pizza Now

The highest-selling brand on the Byte app for a year, driving 50–60% of daily sales.

What I did

Results
  • #1 selling brand on the Byte app for 1 year

The short version

Byte wanted to develop a mid-tier brand with the purpose of selling affordable decent quality pizzas to a Pakistani audience that has a limited budget. To successfully create a brand with these qualities, the design of the brand needed to reflect the "mid-tier aesthetic" that audiences typically/ usually associate with mid-tier affordable brands. Basic colours that are typically associated with pizza shops (red, white, green) combined with serif fonts helped to create the perfect aesthetic and sell the brand making it the most popular brand on the app.

01 / ContextAbout the client

Byte Technology Systems was a Y-Combinator funded food delivery start-up based in Lahore, Pakistan. The start-up sought to utilize smartphone apps and similar technologies to help deliver good quality food to customers at affordable and accessible prices throughout the city.

02 / The challengeWhat needed solving

Byte wanted to create a brand to sell affordable pizzas to a Pakistani audience that's on a limited budget but want to treat themselves and/ or their families to pizzas. The objective was to create a mid-tier brand that appeals to the majority of fast-food consumers. The visual identity around it had to reflect the type of pizzas commonly available at bakeries and small restaurants. The visuals had to appeal to the target audience.

03 / My roleWhat I did

At the start of the project, the brand did not even have a name. It needed to have the word "pizza" in it and had to have something related to "quick service and delivery". I eventually came up with the name “Pizza Now”. For the visuals, I determined the look and feel of the whole brand and developed the visual identity including logos, colour palettes, typography, slogans, patterns. Additionally, I designed the social media posts and also came up with the slogans that would be the voice of the brand.

04 / ApproachHow I worked it out

I started the design process by researching on mid-tier logo styles that mirrored the look and feel that the brand needed to express. I created mood-boards and reference boards that compiled different ideas and visual directions. Then I used the research to iterate the logos and brand identity. The colour palette was influenced by the Italian Flag (as pizza originated in Italy). These colours are also typically associated with pizza and small pizza shops. Since the brand had an aspirational nature to it, a serif font was used for the logo.

05 / ResultsWhat happened

A complete visual identity was designed that included the logo (with variations), colour palette, typography and photography references/ guides. The packaging design also needed to be in line with the aesthetics of the brand and the slogans were added to the packaging to give the brand its voice.

This was a unique design challenge as the objective was to "not make it look too good". Mid-tier aesthetics are different than high-end which is why they need to be designed accordingly. The design and visuals needed to reflect the target audience it was being designed for. The audience needed to relate to it and create an association with it. In this case, going with the norm was the good choice.
