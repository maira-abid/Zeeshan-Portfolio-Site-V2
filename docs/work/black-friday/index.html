The short version

Multiverse wanted to stand out on Black Friday. A turquoise-on-black campaign across email and social lifted product sales by 60–70%.

01 / ContextAbout the client

Multiverse is a brand that provides unique, high quality artistic cannabis smoking products and accessories across North America with a focus on aesthetics, function and price. Their portfolio consists of three brands, Milkyway, Milkyway X and Wormhole, through which they aim to create collectable works of art, prized for their unique style, quality and craftsmanship.

02 / The challengeWhat needed solving

The goal of the project was to utilize and take advantage of the yearly time for Black Friday Sales across North America to market and advertise products at a time when people are looking for good deals to spend money on. The goal was to sell more products and generate more revenue. The campaign was targeted towards the brand's current customers as well as new customers.

03 / My roleWhat I did

As the brand's designer, I came up with the art direction and design for the key visuals of the marketing and sales material that included marketing emails and social media posts.

04 / ApproachHow I worked it out

For the visuals, I introduced a new color palette and typography style. Common colours such as red had been done a lot previously, whereas turquoise was unfamiliar to the brand yet aligned with the brand values.

The design used the texture of the products as backgrounds, contrary to having them at the forefront. The typeface was decorated with colours and strokes to make it visually appealing to the audience. The % of the discount needed to be emphasized as it was the main selling point.
