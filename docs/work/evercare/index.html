The short version

In the early days of COVID-19 people were confused by too much or too little information. Four calm explainer animations averaged around 20K views and cut common queries by 40%.

01 / ContextAbout the client

As an esteemed hospital and healthcare brand present in 6 countries, Evercare aims to provide accessible high quality health and medical care for low and middle income patients in countries with emerging markets.

WatchThe Animations

02 / The challengeWhat needed solving

As the COVID-19 pandemic started to spread quickly in Lahore and other major cities of Pakistan, there was an up-rise in cases across the country, causing panic amongst the public. People did not understand what COVID-19 was, how to avoid it and what to do if they contract it. People were confused by either an excess of information or a lack of information about COVID-19.

03 / My roleWhat I did

I designed the art direction for the explainer animations using the brand guidelines and brand manuals that they had provided. The design of the animations also included all the characters and backgrounds. I also animated 4 different videos, with each animation explaining a different topic related to COVID-19.

04 / ApproachHow I worked it out

The client emphasized on visually communicating the branding of the hospital in the videos. The colours and typography of the brand needed to be at the forefront. The brand had previously used vectors illustrations and characters to communicate to its audience. I had previously used these design elements for their social media posts and graphics. Backgrounds were kept simple to avoid clutter and to focus more on the characters and the animated text. A calm background music was added to relax and de-stress the viewer.

05 / ResultsWhat happened

The animations were positively received by the client and were put up on the hospital's social media. Animations are an effective medium to explain a complex topic in a much simpler way, and get far more traction on social media platforms. They help the viewer understand complex topics easily, so that they can take preventative measures accordingly. They also help the brand develop a better reputation with its audience.
